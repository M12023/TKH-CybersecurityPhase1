QUESTIONS:
1. What Nmap flag did you use to find the specific software versions running on these ports?
Answer: The -sV flag was used due to that flag in particular being responsible for service version detection
it determines what open ports and versions are available.

2. Why is it dangerous to run an intense scan (like -A) on a fragile legacy system?
Answer: Intense scans send a high volume of packets and complex probes (like OS fingerprinting and scripts) in a short amount of time.
 Fragile or legacy systems may have limited processing power or outdated TCP/IP stacks that can't handle the "noise,"
 leading to service crashes, system hangs, or a Denial of Service (DoS) condition.
